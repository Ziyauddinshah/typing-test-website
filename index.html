Professional Typing Rules

  • Space Submits: Hitting Space immediately checks and locks the current word.
  • Color Coded: Correct words turn green; misspelled words turn red.
  • Strict Backspace: Backspace only corrects the current word. It will not delete into previously submitted words.
  • Live Character Feedback: Active letters turn red as soon as a typo is entered.
🇮🇳

हिन्दी टाइपिंग (Hindi Guide)

  • Supports all standard Hindi keyboards: Inscript, Remington (CBE), and Hindi Phonetic / Transliteration.
  • Full support for संयुक्ताक्षर (conjuncts), मात्राएँ, and हलन्त (्).
  • Uses high-clarity Noto Sans Devanagari font for maximum legibility.
  • Word-by-word space submission works identically for Hindi words.
📊

Calculation Standards

  • Net WPM: (Correct Characters / 5) ÷ Elapsed Minutes (Official Standard).
  • Gross WPM: (Total Typed Characters / 5) ÷ Elapsed Minutes.
  • Accuracy: (Correct Words / Total Submitted Words) × 100%.
  • Supports 5, 10, 15, and 30-minute endurance typing evaluations.
